# Welcome to the Ultimate Sports Optimization Platform

The **Fixtures and Squad Optimizer** is a state-of-the-art sports management system designed to bring professional-grade tools to your fingertips. Whether you want to generate a massive league schedule from scratch or mathematically find the absolute best squad for your fantasy sports team, this system does it all with style and precision.

Here is a comprehensive, plain-English breakdown of everything this system can do and exactly how you can use it.

---

## 🚀 Core Feature 1: The Fixture & Tournament Generator
*Have you ever wondered how complex football leagues schedule their matches without having teams play 5 away games in a row? Our system does exactly that.*

This feature allows you to create mathematically perfect schedules for real-world leagues, custom leagues, and even complex tournament formats like the UEFA Champions League. 

### What it can do:
- **Round-Robin League Scheduling:** Generates full home-and-away schedules for leagues like the Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, etc.
- **Custom Leagues:** Want to create your own league? You can pick any number of teams (minimum 10) and it will automatically figure out how many weeks are needed.
- **Advanced Constraints:** It strictly ensures fairness (e.g., no team plays 3 away games in a row, handles winter breaks, avoids scheduling big derbies on the same weekend).
- **The "Swiss Model" (UCL):** Simulates the brand new, highly complex UEFA Champions League format where teams play 8 different opponents based on seeding pots.
- **Visual TV Guides:** Automatically assigns broadcasters (like Sky Sports or TNT) and realistic kickoff times (e.g., Saturday 12:30 PM, Sunday 4:30 PM).
- **Calendar Export:** You can download the generated fixtures directly into your Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, or Outlook (iCal format).
- **3D Bracket Views:** View your tournaments in an interactive, cinematic 3D space.

### 📝 Step-by-Step: How to Use the Fixture Generator
1. **Enter the System:** On the main menu, click **"Fixture Generator"**.
2. **Select a League:** Scroll through the cinematic League Carousel and click on the league you want to schedule (e.g., Premier League, Custom, or Champions League).
3. **Pick Your Teams (Customization):** 
   - A grid of available teams will appear. 
   - Click to manually select the teams you want in your league, or use the **"Auto Select"** button to let the system randomly pick teams for you. 
   - *Pro-Tip:* You can use the **"Tier" Mode** button to see teams arranged by their strength (Pot 1, Pot 2, etc.).
4. **Generate:** Once you've selected enough teams (e.g., at least 10 for a custom league), click the **"Generate Fixtures"** button.
5. **View the Results:**
   - **List View:** Scroll through the weeks to see who plays who.
   - **TV Schedule:** Click the TV icon to see kickoff times and broadcasting channels.
   - **3D View:** If applicable, click the 3D button for an interactive visual.
   - **Export:** Click the "Export to Calendar" button to download your `.ics` calendar file.

## 🧠 Core Feature 2: The Squad Optimizer (Fantasy Sports)
*Fantasy sports is a game of math. This tool uses advanced algorithms (Knapsack Optimization) to solve that math for you, building the statistically perfect team within your budget.*

This tool analyzes thousands of players, their prices, and their projected points to spit out the best possible combination of players.

### What it can do:
- **Football (e.g., FPL):** Picks a 15-man squad (11 starters, 4 bench players) adhering to exact positional rules (e.g., 2 Goalkeepers, 5 Defenders, 5 Midfielders, 3 Forwards).
- **Cricket (e.g., PSL / IPL):** Seamlessly switches logic to pick an 11-man cricket squad based on exact cricket roles (Batsmen, Bowlers, All-rounders, Wicketkeepers).
- **Budget Management:** Strictly adheres to your maximum virtual bankroll (e.g., £100m).
- **Team Limits:** Ensures you don't pick more than the allowed number of players from a single real-world team (e.g., max 3 players from Arsenal).
- **Formation Flexibility:** Automatically figures out the best valid formation (like 3-4-3 or 4-4-2) based on the players it selected.

### 📝 Step-by-Step: How to Use the Squad Optimizer
1. **Enter the System:** On the main menu, click **"Squad Optimizer"**.
2. **Set Your Parameters:**
   - **Sport/League:** Ensure the correct sport is active (Football or Cricket).
   - **Budget:** Adjust the slider to set your maximum budget (e.g., 100m).
   - **Wildcard Mode:** Toggle this on if you want the system to ignore current transfer penalties and just build the best possible team from scratch.
3. **Run the AI:** Click the **"Optimize Squad"** button.
4. **Review the Masterpiece:**
   - The screen will display a beautiful football pitch (or cricket field) layout.
   - Your **Starting XI** will be positioned on the field in the optimal formation.
   - Your **Bench** will be listed on the side, ordered by priority.
   - Look at the top stats bar to see the total cost and the **Projected Points**—this is the mathematical maximum points you can expect!
